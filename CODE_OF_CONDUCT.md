# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone contributing to the Mergington High School website.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat everyone with respect and kindness.
- Give constructive feedback and accept suggestions professionally.
- Be patient and supportive when answering questions.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.
- Respect different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, threatening, or disrespectful comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Disrupting discussions or deliberately making collaboration difficult.

## Reporting Issues

Report inappropriate behavior to the project maintainers or school project administrators. Reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially where possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action when these guidelines are violated.

Thank you for helping maintain a friendly and collaborative environment.
